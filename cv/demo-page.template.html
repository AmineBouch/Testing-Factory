Testing Factory

An AI agent that takes a user story from Jira and does the whole testing job on its own: test plan, test cases, automation, execution and report.

What the agent does, step by step

  1. Reads the user story or bug in Jiraand writes the test plan for it.
  2. Creates the test cases in Xraywith steps, plus a Gherkin scenario for each one.
  3. Automates them with PlaywrightTypeScript and playwright-bdd, straight from the Gherkin.
  4. Runs them in a real browserevery day at 11:00, with no one at the keyboard.
  5. Reports the resulta PDF report and a short video, sent on Telegram and by email. Each failure comes with its screenshot and an analysis.
